## Scope

All six pages of the HRANOL demo (domu, vyroba, stroje, reference, o-firme, poptavka) plus shared chrome (header, footer with the closing call, search palette). Mode: Persuade. Replacement world (2026-10-23, after the "Veletržní stánek" round was judged templated); content, facts and working tools stay. The live three.js flange is dropped from the hero: it duplicated SETINA's signature and told a buyer nothing.

## Audience and job

Portfolio visitors judging range against SETINA first; inside the fiction, buyers and design engineers with a drawing who need fit, capacity and the RFQ path in seconds.

## Direction contract

THESIS: HRANOL is shown in the colours steel itself takes when heated, the náběhové barvy every machinist knows: straw, gold, bronze, purple, blue. The page is straw, the ink is temper blue, the photographs are graded between the two. It refuses the category default of a white page, a stock CNC hero and three service cards, and the previous round's grid of flat panels.

OWN-WORLD: Straw ground #EDB93C frames every page (header, page heads, interludes, footer); content sits on one continuous white sheet inset in the straw, with square corners. Ink is temper blue #151B33, secondary text on straw is dark bronze. Photography from several Pexels photographers graded to one look: cool hues drained toward blue steel, warm hues (sparks, glow, brass, skin) kept, shadows on the ink. Part renders lit by a straw key and a blue fill. Anybody (wide, 800) for lettering, Archivo for text and tabular data. The full temper ramp appears as a UI colour in one place only: the capacity board, where load runs from straw (free) to blue (booked); the same colours appear once as material, in the heat-tint weld render on the home page.

STORY: The visitor reads one plain claim, sees a welder at the bore of a machined casting, and drops a drawing on the white counter beside it; the files ride into the request form. Below: what HRANOL makes, the one-roof weldment sequence beside a plate of a TIG weld showing the real temper colours, eight weeks of capacity as a heat strip, one case with numbers, the four steps, and a close that is the footer itself.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Straw ground. Logo, nav, search and an ink Poptávka button on the straw. The H1 "Přesné díly a svařence pro výrobce strojů." in Anybody up to 6rem; adaptation: it sets in three lines at 1440 (the Czech claim is too long for two lines at display size, and shrinking it would lose the scale). The lead and the links (machine park, next free week) sit in the right column, cols 8 to 12, the same column every section head uses for its aside. Below, the graded hero photograph spans the content width, cropped so the gloves and the bore sit inside the first 1440x900 viewport, with its top-right corner chamfered 45° like the logo (the only chamfer on the site). The white "Máte výkres?" counter stands at the photo's left over the dark casting and rises into the straw under the H1, with the 48-hour line, a live drop zone and a straw "Nahrát výkres" button. Signature interaction: dragging a file anywhere over the window arms the counter; dropping it carries the files into step 1 of the RFQ. Motion grammar: state changes only, on one exponential ease-out, plus cross-document view transitions between pages; no scroll reveals.

FORM: Náběhové barvy (temper colours of steel), position 4 on the ordered list (assigned), seed:a0c832520324b68e. Raises from declined challengers: from the memory quilt, every photograph carries its caption and its photographer's credit; from the design annual's plate section, photographs run at plate scale, never as thumbnails in cards; from the toyism plate, the palette is fixed and nothing outside it is introduced, no gradients except the one ramp; from the particle event display, hovering a capacity cell isolates its technology row and week column; from the suminagashi basin, the ramp keeps the real physical order of the temper colours and is never shuffled for decoration; from Versailles, every page holds one left axis that headlines, leads and sheet content share.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Red lines: not artsy, no dark tech look (dark appears only inside photographs), no blueprint gimmicks (title blocks, stamps, mono caps labels), nothing of SETINA (enamel green #1E2F28, Sofia Sans, green-graded photos, keyed-shaft logo). No eyebrows, no all-caps, no arrows in link text, no em dashes in copy. WCAG 2.2 AA, self-hosted fonts, noindex, fictional-firm note in header strip and footer.
