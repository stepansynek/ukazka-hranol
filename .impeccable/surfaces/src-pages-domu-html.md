## Scope

All six pages of the HRANOL demo (domu, vyroba, stroje, reference, o-firme, poptavka) plus shared chrome (fascia header, footer, search palette). Mode: Persuade. Replacement world; content, facts and working tools stay.

## Audience and job

Portfolio visitors judging range against SETINA first; inside the fiction, buyers and design engineers with a drawing who need fit, capacity and the RFQ path in seconds.

## Direction contract

THESIS: HRANOL presents itself as its own stand at the Brno engineering fair: a frontal elevation of a signal-yellow system wall with the claim lettered on it, the work shown as exhibits on plinths, and a counter where you hand over a drawing. It refuses the category default of a white page, a stock hero photo and a grid of same-size service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Signal yellow #F5C400 walls, graphite #1C1E21 lettering and counter tops, aluminium #C9CED3 grooved posts and rails, white counters, plinths and fascia, warm hall-floor grey #E4E4DF as the page ground. Archivo 800 at width 125 for lettering, Archivo 87 width with tabular figures for data. Square edges, posts between equal 1 m panels, soft real shadows only where a counter or plinth stands in front of the wall. Home/away rule: yellow walls persuade, white counters work; on white, yellow marks the free and the actionable.

STORY: The visitor sees a real shop's stand, reads one claim per panel, turns the part on its plinth, and drops a drawing on the counter; the files ride into the request form. Below, counters prove capacity, fit and machines; the close is another wall with the counter line.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White fascia with aluminium rails (logo, nav, search, graphite Poptávka). Under it a yellow wall split by posts at 2/3: the left 2 m panel carries the H1 lettered at ~5.8vw over three lines, the lead and a line button; the 3D flange turns above a white plinth with its exhibit label, standing at the lower right of that panel. The right panel carries "Máte výkres?" lettered above a white counter with a graphite top: 48-hour line, live drop zone, yellow Nahrát výkres button. Hall floor below with the free-capacity status line. Signature interaction: dragging a file anywhere over the window lights the counter; dropping it there carries the files into step 1 of the RFQ. Motion grammar: one authored moment, page changes as a sideways step along the stand (cross-document view transitions, exponential ease-out), plus the exhibit's own turn; everything else is state, not decoration.

FORM: The fair stand (Veletržní stánek), position 3 on the ordered list (assigned), seed:5500e6023e4c3d09.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment
