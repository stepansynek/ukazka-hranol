# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, in this order:

1. **Portfolio visitors.** Owners and managers of small technical firms around Brno, Vyškov and Rousínov who look at Štěpán Synek's "Web na klíč" offer on stepansynek.com and open this demo to judge what he could build for them. The site sits next to a sibling demo (SETINA) and has to prove range: a visitor who sees both must read them as two different studios' worth of work, not one template recolored.
2. **The fictional firm's buyers**, the audience the copy is written for. Purchasing staff and design engineers at machine makers in Czechia, Germany and Austria (packaging lines, agricultural machinery, robotics, hydraulics). They arrive with a drawing (PDF plus STEP), need to know quickly whether HRANOL can make the part, when, and with what documentation, and then send a request for quote. Mostly at a desk in an office, sometimes on a phone in the hall.

## Product Purpose

A believable website for HRANOL s.r.o., a fictional job shop in Rousínov: CNC milling, turning, cylindrical grinding, welding and sub-assembly for machine makers, from one prototype to series of thousands. Success for the portfolio: a prospective client believes a real machine shop could launch it tomorrow, and remembers it as clearly different from SETINA. Success inside the fiction: a buyer finds capability, free capacity and the RFQ path within seconds and uploads a drawing.

## Positioning

What HRANOL can truthfully say that a neighboring job shop could not copy as written:

- Machining, welding, measuring and assembly under one roof in one 80 × 40 m hall; weldments are annealed and then machined in one clamping, so bearing seats line up without straightening.
- Free capacity is published per technology for the next eight weeks.
- Price and lead time within 48 hours of a drawing, quoted by a technician; NDA signed before documents are sent.
- A measuring protocol and a 3.1 material certificate with every delivery; climate-controlled metrology room at 20 ± 1 °C.

## Operating Context

The buyer's path: drawing in hand → can you make it (envelope, materials, tolerance) → when (capacity, lead time) → who quotes it and how fast → upload drawings and send the RFQ. Supporting questions: references in the same industry, certification (ISO 9001, EN ISO 3834-2, EN ISO 9606-1), people to call directly, jobs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Six static pages generated by `python3 build.py` from `src/pages/*.html` plus data in `build.py` (capacity, machines, hall plan, search index). `--spa OUT` builds a single-file preview. Generated HTML is never hand-edited.
- Working tools that must keep working: rotatable 3D part (three.js) in the home hero with a static fallback; free-capacity table that rolls its weeks from the current date; "Will your part fit?" checker that marks machines; SVG hall floor plan with zones linked to machines; ⌘K / Ctrl K site search; three-step RFQ form with file upload, live summary and per-step validation; View Transitions between pages.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages at https://stepansynek.com/ukazka-hranol/ (repo stepansynek/ukazka-hranol, branch deploy, `.nojekyll`). `noindex`, and the firm is labeled fictional in a top strip and the footer.
- Czech language with Czech typographic rules (non-breaking spaces after one-letter prepositions and between numbers and units; `build.py` applies them).
- Scope of the 2026-10-22 redesign: look and layout may change completely; all pages, facts, copy and working tools stay.

## Brand Commitments

- Name HRANOL s.r.o., Rousínov u Brna, founded 1994, family firm in its second generation, 46 people, 3 200 m² hall. The user allowed a new logo and identity in this redesign.
- Voice: plain, factual Czech with concrete numbers and named machines; no hype.
- Binding red lines from the user: not too artsy for a machine shop (a purchasing engineer must trust it); no dark "tech" look (near-black with glowing accents); no blueprint or technical-drawing gimmicks (title blocks, stamps, monospace caps labels); nothing that reads as SETINA's template recolored.
- Sibling and frame to stay distinct from: SETINA (`~/setina-web`, being rebuilt in parallel) and stepansynek.com itself (white paper, blue ink #1f3c9e, orange plates, Special Gothic).

## Evidence on Hand

- Copy, data and contacts in `src/pages/` and `build.py`; all of it is fictional (README states this).
- Photos: six Unsplash images in `assets/img/` (milling, turning, welding, assembly, measuring, grinding wheels), license in README.
- Own three.js renders of delivered parts (`ref-hvezdice`, `ref-ram`, `ref-celisti`, `ref-pistnice`, `priruba`) and the live 3D flange in `assets/part.js`.
- No real customers, testimonials, certificates or photos of an actual hall exist; nothing may be presented as real.

## Product Principles

1. Prove, don't claim: every page carries one working element a buyer can use (capacity, fit check, hall plan, RFQ).
2. Believable first: a real Moravian job shop could publish this unchanged, apart from the fictional-firm label.
3. Range is the portfolio point: HRANOL must share no visual grammar with SETINA.
4. The drawing is the conversion: the path to uploading a drawing is never more than one click away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the working standard: keyboard paths through the search palette, the fit checker, the hall plan and the form; visible focus; reduced-motion support; tables with captions and scoped headers.
